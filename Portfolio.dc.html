06.
Rebranding Local street food 
Print and E-media production - Group project
⌄

We have tried the Thai dessert called "ไข่เต่าชีส" or "Kai Tao" and found it delicious; While the shop may already be well-known, it currently lacks a distinctive identity or an eye-catching aesthetic that would encourage customers to take photos and share them on social media, thereby raising awareness of this unique treat. We visited the actual shop location near Talat Phlu to better understand the brand and the surrounding atmosphere. We observed that during peak hours (in the evening), the shop attracts a large number of students. With an average price of 3 baht per piece (with no cheese), we aim to revitalise the shop's image to better appeal to its primary customer base while maintaining a "vintage-meets-contemporary" style that complements the location.

The food cart is located next to a main road in a busy community area with constant foot traffic. A disadvantage, however, is that nearby stores have brightly lit signs that obscure the cart's visibility
